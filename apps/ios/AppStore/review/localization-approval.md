# Localization approval

| Store locale | App resources | Product | Legal | Professional language review | State |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `en-US` | `en` | Pending | Pending | Native copy review pending | Draft |
| `es-MX` | `es` | Pending | Pending | Mexican Spanish review pending | Draft |

Static validation enforces app/package key parity and checks prohibited or overly
complex copy. That is not a translation approval. Long-tail feature screens, domain
fixtures, legal notices, TestFlight instructions, screenshots, and App Store metadata
must be reviewed together against the exact build.

Record approver, date, source revision, translated revision, unresolved terminology,
and screenshot evidence before changing either state to Approved.
