Alpha 0.1 is restricted to internal TestFlight and is not eligible for public App Review.

The binary is labeled “Internal demo · Do not use real information” and uses local fixture data. It demonstrates onboarding, document organization, capture entry points, missing-item answer modes, human review, accessibility preferences, offline behavior, and package safety blockers. Camera, Photos, and Files system pickers may be opened, but reviewers must not enter personal information.

The app intentionally refuses production mode while StubAPIClient is compiled. Passkeys, production account deletion, off-device document processing, voice services, and final secure delivery require the production API/authentication release.

No review credentials are committed to the repository. A future public submission must provide a non-expiring review account or an approved fully featured review mode, an always-available backend, published privacy/support URLs, and exact testing instructions in App Store Connect.
